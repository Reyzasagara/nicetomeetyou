On a team

How I work with a team

The order I usually work in, from the first conversation to the next round of changes.

  1. Understand what the business needs
  2. Talk to the people doing the work
  3. Map the current process from real documents
  4. Find where errors and delays start
  5. Design the new workflow
  6. Turn it into requirements and rules
  7. Get business and tech teams on the same page
  8. Test with UAT and real system data
  9. Measure adoption and results
  10. Keep improving, including removing what doesn't work
