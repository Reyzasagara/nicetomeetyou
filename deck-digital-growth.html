In plain terms

Adverts brought 697 people to WhatsApp in 19 days. 116 of them were never answered at all. 204 were answered, but nobody asked the one question that decides which dealer they belong to. And 49 of the people most ready to buy never reached a dealer. None of that needed more budget.

Two decisions I took to the team
  • 01Stop advertising in provinces where no dealer can serve the buyer — 45 conversations came from there.
  • 02Make one promo wording true everywhere; three buyers were told at the dealer that the advert's offer did not apply.

Both cost nothing to fix, and both stayed invisible until the conversations were read.
