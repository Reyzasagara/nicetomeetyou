On a team

How I work with a team

The order I usually work in, from the first conversation to the next round of changes.

  1. Walk the line and follow one unit
  2. List every record that gets created or re-typed
  3. Find the post where each value is born
  4. Design the capture: label, photo, scan or list
  5. Add checks at that post
  6. Link every record to one unit identity
  7. Give PPIC and QC a live view
  8. Fix whatever operators keep working around
