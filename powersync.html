02 / Rules between modules

What one module changes in another

From the requirements spec. Each rule keeps a step from happening out of order.

  1. Finishing a pre-delivery inspection moves the order to received.

    Pre-delivery Quality InspectionOrder Management

  2. Every NG part found in inspection becomes a delivery claim candidate once the unit is delivered.

    Pre-delivery Quality InspectionDelivery Claims

  3. An order can't be marked delivered until delivery prep is complete and inspection is done.

    Delivery PreparationPre-delivery Quality InspectionOrder Management

  4. An order can't be billed without a confirmed payment proof.

    Billing & PaymentOrder Management

  5. Each registration request belongs to one chassis number, and the BBN faktur prints only after the order is complete.

    Legal Request (vehicle registration)VIN / Production TrackerOrder Management

  6. An approved warranty replacement creates a linked replacement order.

    KSG Warranty ClaimsOrder Management

  7. Kiosk output and PPIC parameters build the daily production report.

    Production Kiosk & MESManufacturing / PPICE-LHP & Finished Goods

  8. A unit with a critical NG in in-process QC can't move on until the repair is verified.

    Internal QC (in-process)Production Kiosk & MES

  9. A meeting action item can become a project.

    Meeting MinutesWorkload / Project Management

  10. A sales area manager only sees the dealers assigned to them, in every module.

01Order ManagementDealer vehicle orders, from pending to complete.4 features
The problem it solves

Orders sent by letter, email and WhatsApp with no shared status and no record of who approved what.

Features

  • Order with unit configuration
    What it does

    A dealer orders one or more units and picks base, body, cabin, colour and accessories.

    What it solves

    Invalid combinations get blocked at entry instead of being found at the factory.

  • Ten statuses with permissions
    What it does

    Pending, received, approved, confirmed, processed, delivered, billed, complete, plus cancelled. Each move needs the right permission.

    What it solves

    Nobody can skip a step, such as jumping from pending straight to delivered.

  • Audit trail and notifications
    What it does

    Every status change records old and new values, user and time, and notifies the people involved.

    What it solves

    Arguments about who changed an order can be settled from the log.

  • Delivery address by region
    What it does

    Province, city and district dropdowns built on Indonesian region data.

    What it solves

    Address typos that break delivery and registration documents.

02Pre-delivery Quality InspectionAn 85+ point checklist for every unit before it ships.4 features
The problem it solves

Defects found by the dealer after delivery, with no proof of the unit's condition when it left.

Features

  • Checklist by category and part
    What it does

    Each unit is checked part by part as OK, NG or N/A, with notes and photos.

    What it solves

    Inspections that depended on memory and paper forms.

  • NG with photo proof
    What it does

    A part marked NG needs a defect category and at least one photo.

    What it solves

    Arguments later about whether a defect was there before shipping.

  • Completion rules
    What it does

    An inspection can only finish when every part is classified, and a finished inspection can't be edited.

    What it solves

    Half-filled checklists and results changed after the fact.

  • Inspection report PDF
    What it does

    Generates a report with the full checklist, NG photos and the inspector's signature.

    What it solves

    Re-typing results into a document for the dealer.

The problem it solves

Delivery complaints sent as photos in chat, with no status and no link to what inspection found.

Features

  • Claim with photo evidence
    What it does

    Missing details that slow the review down.

    What it does

    Submitted, under review, approved or rejected, resolved. Claims are accepted only for a set period after delivery.

  • Bulk review
    What it does

    Approve or reject several claims at once with a shared comment.

    What it solves

    Handling claims one by one when many units arrive together.

04KSG Warranty ClaimsFree-service and warranty claims per unit, with a QC review.4 features
The problem it solves

Quarterly batches of paper coupons reconciled by hand in Excel.

Features

  • Warranty period from the delivery date
    What it does

    The warranty window is worked out from the delivery date, 12 months by default.

    What it solves

    Claims approved for units already out of warranty.

  • Evidence on every claim
    What it does

    Photos, short videos and documents are attached to each claim, including mileage and a speedometer photo.

    What it solves

    Claims nobody could check because the proof lived on someone's phone.

  • QC review through to replacement
    What it does

    Draft, submitted, QC review, approved, replacement processed, closed. An approved replacement creates a linked replacement order.

    What it solves

    Replacements promised but never tracked as an order.

  • Bulk creation and defect reports
    What it does

    Create many claims from an Excel template; monthly, quarterly and yearly reports by defect type.

    What it solves

    No view of which defects keep coming back.
