On a team

How I work with a team

The order I usually work in, from the first conversation to the next round of changes.

  1. Write down the business question
  2. List the sources and where they came from
  3. Clean and join by region and period
  4. Build the model and state the assumptions
  5. Check the conclusion holds in both scenarios
  6. Turn the insight into a recommendation
  7. Hand it to someone who owns the program
  8. Check what changed
