On a team

How I work with a team

The order I usually work in, from the first conversation to the next round of changes.

  1. Read the regulation and the test items
  2. Test ourselves before the official test
  3. Log every NG and its cause
  4. Fix, retest, document
  5. Submit the test package
  6. Validate on real roads with real loads
  7. Hand the harness drawings to production
  8. Feed field findings back into the design
