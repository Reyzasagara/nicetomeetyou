EV Type Approval & Validation: PowerAce TRIEX

I handled the electrical side of an imported electric three-wheeler prototype and prepared its type approval, which ended with a government certificate (SUT, Sep 2023). That included fixing what failed our own pre-test, putting together the test package and filing the second type test in-house.

The situation

After the company's IPO, the three-wheeler project was meant to show that the company could build its own product. The imported electric prototype was not ready for production and did not have type approval.

The goal

Get the prototype's electrical system ready and take the vehicle through government type approval.

What I did

  1. Ran our own pre-test (14 Mar 2023), which found NG items (gear indicator, parking sensor, cover rubber) before the official test could.
  2. Prepared the test package: QC self-assessment, SNI 8872 battery test and specifications.
  3. Filed the second type test and paid the government fee in-house (IDR 9.7 million, Jun 2023).
  4. Compared EV controllers (Votol, Fardriver, Arduino-based) and set up commissioning in the field using the supplier's guide.
  5. Planned road tests with Google Earth gradient profiles, then ran slope, water, charging and range tests.
  6. Drew the EV wiring harness for mass production (Jul 2024).

What changed

  • SUT type-approval certificate issued 15 Sep 2023.
  • Passed a 25% slope test with 800 kg and a 270 mm water test with everything still working.
  • Full charge in 4 h 35 min and a 59 km range (Jun 2024).
  • Mass-production harness drawing released Jul 2024 and revised in 2025.

What I don't claim

And why
  • A first-attempt pass score.
  • Savings compared with consultant fees. The evidence shows a direct government fee.
  • Finished tests on the 37% and 47% gradient routes. They were planned.
  • Writing the controller commissioning guide. It's the supplier's document.
  • The frame and body design. That was my team's work; my part was the electrical system.

What I learned

Passing type approval didn't mean the product fit the market. At two to four times the price of competing EVs, with a 59 km range, it didn't yet do the job most buyers needed, and that's why I moved into market research.

Every claim on this page traces to a verified evidence item. The source files are internal company documents, and I'm happy to walk through them in an interview.
