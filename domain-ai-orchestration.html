On a team

How I work with a team

The order I usually work in, from the first conversation to the next round of changes.

  1. Find work that rules alone can't handle
  2. Split fixed steps from AI steps
  3. Decide what context and sources the AI gets
  4. Design the agent and tool workflow
  5. Add human approval where mistakes are expensive
  6. Decide how to judge the output
  7. Make sure the process still works when AI is off
  8. Tune cost, speed and reliability
