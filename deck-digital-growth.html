How I deliver it: collect, read, hand over

The same three steps on every project. The reading step is the one people skip.

Step 1

Collect

What the funnel is made of

  • 01Tag every WhatsApp click with GTM and GA4 events
  • 02Export ad spend and reach per post from Meta Ads
  • 03Export the WhatsApp conversations for the same period
  • 04List which areas an active dealer actually covers

Output: One week of conversations, joined to spend and area

Step 2

Read

One week, conversation by conversation

  • 01Read all 328 conversations instead of sampling
  • 02Tag each one with what stopped it
  • 03Group the tags into leak types that repeat
  • 04Separate what we control from what we don't

Output: Four leak types, counted, not guessed

Step 3

Hand over

So it becomes someone's job

  • 01Write each leak type as a requirement with a rule
  • 02Brief an AI follow-up draft that a person approves
  • 03Make dealer coverage a check before ad spend
  • 04State plainly what is still not tracked

Output: A requirement, a brief, and an honest list of gaps

Support AI orchestrationDraft the follow-up; a person still approves every reply.
Support Operations digitalizationThe dealer side of the funnel runs in PowerSync.

What I don't claim, and what I'd build next
