PowerSync: Manufacturing, PPIC & Shop-Floor Traceability

Scope and terminology

Operational figures describe reviewed records, not guaranteed results for all periods. Unit totals may include legacy records; the import breakdown and the 217-pair review period are not confirmed.

Faktur: vehicle registration certificate request. SUT: government vehicle type approval certificate. NOKA/NOSIN: frame and engine numbers.

The situation

Engine (NOSIN) and frame (NOKA) numbers were planned in spreadsheets, stamped by hand and often found wrong too late: on invoices, on registration documents or after delivery. Across 4,818 unit records, 103 shared 51 engine numbers.

Production reports and PDI checklists were on paper, so PPIC and QC saw the line a day late.

The goal

Catch number errors at the post where they happen, and give PPIC and QC a live view of the line.

My responsibilities

  1. Planned frame numbers at recap time, skipping numbers already used.
  2. Designed kiosks for the assembly and repair posts: a photo of the stamped number, and problem lists to pick from instead of typing.
  3. Added format checks plus a normalised duplicate check (O vs 0, spacing) that catches duplicates exact matching misses.
  4. Printed the planned numbers on QR labels for the line.
  5. Moved QC from a paper checklist to inspections recorded against each unit, with NG units sent through the repair post.
  6. Let PPIC set parameters and create a production recap from a phone. Later added AI that reads handwritten QC forms, while production carries on if the AI is off.

What changed

  • Off-format numbers dropped from 101 to 25 in one scan, with no exact duplicates left.
  • 0 mismatches across 217 planned vs stamped pairs, and 635 units checked by photo.
  • 780 QC inspections recorded; the production report and PDI checklist are no longer on paper.
  • A split PO stays one record: a 4-unit PO shipped on two delivery reports three weeks apart still shows partial delivery against each unit.

What I don't claim

And why
  • A guarantee that all historical records are error-free. Results apply only to the reviewed dataset.
  • A shorter cycle time. It hasn't been measured yet.
  • Accuracy figures for the AI form reading.

What I learned

A check at the kiosk takes a few seconds. The same wrong number found at registration means a reissued document and a customer left waiting.

I built the AI steps so production keeps running when the AI is off or out of quota. The line can't stop and wait for a model.
