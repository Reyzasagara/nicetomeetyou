Market Research & Data Analysis

3W Market Intelligence & Demand Modelling

Sales data from 2022 to Aug 2024 showed that the market leader sold 75.4% of its units in Java. I used that finding, two market-sizing scenarios and a regional score to recommend where dealer expansion should go next.

The situation

The company went into retail three-wheelers without its own market data. Dealer expansion was opportunistic, and a lot of it went to areas far from the factory.

The goal

Answer one question with data: where is the Indonesian three-wheeler market really, and where are the best opportunities?

What I did

  1. Wrote the Strategic Renewal proposal (Sep–Dec 2024) with two market-sizing scenarios and their assumptions spelled out.
  2. Kept a register of 14 data sources, each flagged with where it came from.
  3. Collected public data (BPS regional statistics through a scraper, Google Trends and local surveys) to score pilot areas.
  4. Built a whitespace score (registration gap × regional GDP × access) and Power BI dashboards for market and sales performance.
  5. Grounded customer needs in a 349-response survey, focus groups, car clinics and a customer and dealer journey map.

What changed

  • Our expansion had targeted areas holding about 25% of the market, while the leader sold 75.4% of its units in Java.
  • The 2025 plan adopted a Java dealer focus, an exclusive-dealer scheme and a digitalization objective.
  • The recommendations turned into programs: events in five competitor strongholds, a dealership tier program, a sub-dealer SOP, and the digitalization objective that became PowerSync.

What I don't claim

And why
  • Market share or sales gains from these programs. Their commercial effect hasn't been measured.
  • A full sensitivity analysis beyond the two sizing scenarios.

What I learned

The Java conclusion held in both sizing scenarios, so it didn't depend on which market-size estimate turned out to be right.

The 2025 plan assigned owners to the dealer scheme and PowerSync. That is when the analysis moved from slides into day-to-day work.
